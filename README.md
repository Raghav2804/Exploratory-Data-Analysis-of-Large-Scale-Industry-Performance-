# Exploratory Data Analysis of Large-Scale Industry Performance Dataset

## Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a large-scale industry performance dataset containing 15,000 records and 12 variables. The objective is to analyze company performance, revenue trends, employee distribution, customer behavior, and market-related patterns using statistical and visualization techniques.

---

## Objectives

- Perform Univariate Analysis
- Perform Bivariate Analysis
- Perform Multivariate Analysis
- Detect and Interpret Outliers
- Measure Correlations Between Numerical Variables
- Analyze Skewness and Kurtosis
- Extract Meaningful Business Insights

---

## Dataset Information

### Dataset Size
- Rows: 15,000
- Columns: 12

- id

- annual_revenue_million

## Key Findings

- Revenue distribution is positively skewed.
- Large enterprises contribute significantly to total revenue.
- Technology sector generates the highest average revenue.
- Retail sector handles the highest customer volumes.
- Employee count strongly influences company revenue.
- Customer count has a strong positive relationship with revenue.
- Market rating moderately influences profit margin.
- Outliers represent genuine large-scale organizations and were retained.
- North American companies achieve higher market ratings and profitability.
- Technology companies with larger employee bases generate the highest revenue.

---

## Business Insights

- Workforce size is a major driver of revenue growth.
- Customer base significantly impacts business performance.
- Technology industry dominates revenue generation.
- Retail companies manage the largest customer bases.
- Industry benchmarking can be performed using revenue and employee metrics.
- The analysis supports business intelligence and strategic planning.
